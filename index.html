      Routing preferences

      • Ticket: crossing needs International validity for that day, either an International pass or a one-day upgrade. A Cervinia-only pass doesn't cover the lifts back up from the Swiss side.
      • Italy: third-party liability insurance is mandatory on Italian slopes.
      • Wind: the crossing lifts at Plateau Rosà / Testa Grigia and Plan Maison–Laghi close first in wind. If they're shut in the morning, skip the crossing.
      • Winter 2026/27: Zermatt's timetable lists a revision closure from 7 Jan to 26 Feb 2027 near the Testa Grigia / Glacier Ride II row. If it's shut, mark it closed and the route home will use the Furggsattel chair.
      • Switzerland uses CHF and cards work everywhere. Keep your phone warm, since batteries die fast at 3,500 m.
      • Plateau Rosà / Testa Grigia and the top of the Theodul glacier are long and flat. Carry speed and be ready to unstrap and skate.
      • Turn on Avoid drag lifts and the planner prefers chairs, gondolas and cable cars.
      • Marked runs (blue/red/black) are patrolled. Dotted orange lines are ski routes or freeride, which are not groomed and not for this plan.
      • The Ventina home run is about 11 km. Late in the day it gets choppy, so save your legs.
      • Open the map and tap 🏠 Home. It routes from where you are to your trip's last stop and flags any lift with a last-ride time.
      • If the route home uses a lift that's already past its key time, ask lift staff right away about alternatives.
      • Stuck on the wrong side, you're looking at a roughly 5-hour taxi (around €500) or a night in Zermatt. Treat the cut-offs as hard.
      • Tap the map to go full screen. Pinch to zoom; labels appear as you zoom in.
      • ℹ️ Info: tap any piste or lift for details plus Mark, Done, Closed, I'm here and Go here.
      • 📍 I'm here: tap where you are, or turn on 📡 GPS and the 🏂 follows you automatically (tap 📡 again to stop following, a third time to turn GPS off).
      • 🎯 Go to: tap any point to get turn-by-turn lifts and pistes from your spot.
      • 🖍 Mark: tap trails to highlight them. ➕ Stop: tap to add a stop to the current trip.
      • Make a new trip for any day. Trips, marks and settings stay saved on this phone.

Ski Planner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Ski Planner is a personal trail map and ski-day tracker. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

  • Location: GPS positions while you ride, used to show where you are and to calculate runs, speed and vertical.
  • Apple Health (optional): saves ski days as workouts and reads heart rate for those days.
  • WHOOP (optional): reads workouts, day strain and heart rate (scopes read:workout, read:cycles, offline).
  • Strava (optional): GPX files you choose to export, or uploads you start yourself.

Where it is stored

All data, including connection keys and access tokens, is stored only on your own phone. Nothing is sent to a server run by Ski Planner, and there is no such server. Data is exchanged only directly between your phone and the services you connect (Apple Health, WHOOP, Strava).

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Deleting your data

Use “Disconnect” in the app to remove a connection’s tokens, “Clear today” to delete a day, or delete the app to remove everything. You can also revoke access in your WHOOP, Strava or Apple Health settings at any time.
